Meet VESTA · Virtual Engineer for Space-fire Threat Assessment

An AI crewmate that can't make up numbers

VESTA is a tool-using agent. It picks tools, runs them on NASA data and writes the answer. Then a grounding check scans every number in the reply. Any number that isn't in a tool result or in the user's question gets flagged, and the answer's confidence drops. Pick a question to watch a replay built from FlameScope's real data and formulas.

03 · Benefits

What FlameScope gives its users

Form question: What benefits does it have?
⏱️
1 question

Checking whether a NASA test covered your cabin used to mean reading through technical memos. Now you ask one question and get the matching rows back.

🔗
100% traced

Every number comes from a NASA table or a tested formula, and links to its file and row or page. The formulas are shown on screen.

🕳️
Gaps named

“No data” is a real answer. It says what is missing, shows the closest tests and points to where data may exist.

📡
Works offline

If no AI service is reachable, Offline mode answers with the same tools and no AI, so the app keeps working with no API key.

04 · Intended impact

The next cabins won't breathe
ISS air

For lunar habitats and pressurized rovers, NASA is considering an exploration atmosphere of 34% oxygen at 8.2 psi. That is very different from the air on the ISS, where nearly all of the fire data was gathered. FlameScope shows how much of the evidence carries over and what still needs to be tested.

06 · How it is creative

Five ideas that make it different

Form question: How is your project creative?
🤖

An AI that can't invent numbers

VESTA is a friendly crewmate, but a grounding check stands between it and the user. Every number has to come from a tool result or the user's question.

❔

“No data” is an answer

Most dashboards guess. FlameScope treats an honest gap as a first-class result and turns it into a list of experiments worth running.

🎯

Same-row matching

Instead of averaging tests together, a result counts only if one real NASA test recorded every condition you asked about.

🔵

See gravity change a flame

Earth's teardrop flame sits beside the round, blue microgravity flame, with partial gravity drawn as an uncertain, dashed shape.

🪐

A 3D habitat map

Every test sits in 3D by oxygen, pressure and oxygen partial pressure, set between Earth and the Moon, so gaps can be seen at a glance.

🔔

A crewmate that speaks first

When you switch to a riskier mission, VESTA raises a proactive alert with the hazard first and the sources attached, without calling an AI model.

07 · Factors our team considered

The decisions behind the build

Form question: What factors did your team consider?

Limits FlameScope shows openly

GravityEvery test ran in microgravity. Lunar and Martian gravity are untested, and the app never extrapolates to them.
MaterialsSolid-material evidence covers three materials: acrylic, SIBAL fabric and Nomex. Foams, wiring and enriched-oxygen solids are gaps.
ScoresRanking, severity and the hazard index are FlameScope heuristics with published formulas. They are not NASA ratings.
