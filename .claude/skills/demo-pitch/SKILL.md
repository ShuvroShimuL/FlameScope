# Demo and pitch

The source of truth is `docs/planning/demo-script.md` and `docs/planning/scorecard.md`.

## Rules

- **240 s maximum**, with a beginning (question), a middle (the surprise: Burned → No data) and an end (impact + challenge name).
- **Plain English** for non-native listeners: short sentences, one idea per line, and every term defined once.
- **Name the dataset aloud:** "NASA's BASS-II experiment, technical memo 2021-0011385, Table 5.1."
- **Name the challenge:** "NASA Space Apps 2026: Flame in Freefall."
- **Never say "safe."** Say "burned", "no data", or "the evidence covers / doesn't cover."
- **Impact numbers** (Bangladesh) only if verified with a link (ADR-007).
- Show one surprising interaction: the tap that flips the stamp and turns the flame into a dashed "?".
- Show the Validity argument: the `lib/evidence` folder and its boundary test, the Sources section of Will it burn?, and a source chip under a VESTA answer.

## Project-page checklist

Every field filled in: title, challenge, summary (≤ 3 sentences), how it works, NASA data used (with links), other data (agency or third party), AI use (link to `docs/AI_USE.md`), team roles, repo URL (public), demo video URL.
